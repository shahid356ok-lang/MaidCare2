# 🏠 MaidCare – Maid & Nanny Service Management Platform

## 📌 Project Overview

**MaidCare** is a web-based Maid & Nanny Service Management Platform designed to connect households with domestic helpers such as maids, babysitters, and nannies.

The platform provides a simple and convenient way for users to browse helpers, check their experience and availability, select service plans, and send booking requests.

The project focuses on improving **trust, transparency, reliability, and convenience** in the process of hiring domestic help.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Households often depend on informal contacts or unverified agents when hiring domestic help. This can result in:

* Lack of background verification
* Unreliable service
* Sudden absenteeism
* No standardized pricing
* Poor communication
* Manual coordination and follow-ups

MaidCare aims to provide a centralized platform that makes the hiring process easier and more organized.
